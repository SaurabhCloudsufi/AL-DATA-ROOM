Anthropic — Published Visualizations

These reproduce the views Anthropic's own Economic Index presents, rebuilt from the published file rather than screenshotted. The whole domain carries one caveat that cannot be repeated often enough: this is one provider's own consumer traffic, so every share is a share of Claude conversations and never of AI use at large.

It is the file that speaks to enterprise workloads, and it behaves nothing like the consumer one.

Derived Analysis

Our own analysis of the same two files — the published views held at a grain where they vary, and the API set against Claude.ai on the measures where they diverge. The two are never pooled: they are different populations, and the gap between them is the finding. A cell Anthropic did not publish is absent rather than zero, so a missing country is a suppressed one.
