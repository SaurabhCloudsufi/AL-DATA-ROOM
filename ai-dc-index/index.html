Map

One interactive map of the whole index. Circle area is announced capacity as the operator stated it, not measured delivered load, and the year slider is cumulative — it shows which sites had started by a year, not how any one site grew. Where the source gives no coordinates the site is geocoded once to the finest level its record supports, and anything coarser than an exact position is drawn with an uncertainty halo rather than a false rooftop.

DCMAP-01

AI data centres worldwide, by announced capacity

Every site in the AI DC INDEX placed on an interactive world map, sized by announced IT capacity and colourable by company, build status, energy source or AI workload. The year slider is cumulative: it shows which sites had started by each year.

Dataset
AI DC INDEX (AI DC INDEX.xlsx, eight continent sheets)
Granularity
One record per site; announced capacity and a single start year, no capacity time series
Coverage
346 sites across 64 countries; start years 2000-2032, slider floors at 2015
Source files
AI_DC_INDEX.xlsx + geocode_cache.csv
Data treatment
Announced capacity as stated by the source; 52 of 346 rows carry no coordinates and are geocoded once to city, region or country precision, recorded per row and drawn with an uncertainty halo below exact precision
Source
AI DC INDEX
Methodology
Methodology reference pending final methodology document · Free-text status, energy and workload columns are classified on their head clause into closed vocabularies; the original wording is carried through and shown on every site card
Note
circle area encodes announced capacity, which is what operators state, not measured delivered load; 52 sites disclose no capacity figure and are searchable but not drawn
