Epoch AI — Published Visualizations

Epoch publishes one configurable figure on this dataset — a metric against the date it was reported, over a chosen tab, with controls for linear or log scale and a fitted growth regression. These are that figure at each of its settings, rebuilt from the downloaded CSVs rather than screenshotted. Rows Epoch flags 'exclude from graph view' are dropped here exactly as they are there, and the projection control is off throughout.

Derived Analysis

Our own analysis of the same six files — questions the tabs cannot answer, because they need two of them at once: revenue against headcount, valuation against revenue, serving against training. Where two observations are paired, each is matched to the nearest in time within a stated window and unmatched rows are dropped, never stretched to a convenient partner.

COMPANIES-D01

Growth rates the record can support

Doubling time for every series with enough observations to fit, against the count of series that have observations but too few to fit.

Dataset
Epoch AI — AI Companies
Granularity
One fitted series
Year
2013–2026 (observed to 2026-08-13)
Coverage
4 series fitted; 18 with observations below the 12-point threshold
Source files
ai_companies_revenue_reports.csv + ai_companies_staff_reports.csv + ai_companies_usage_reports.csv
Data treatment
Ordinary least squares on log10(metric) against date; a fit is refused below 12 points
Source
Epoch AI — AI Companies
Methodology
Methodology reference pending final methodology document · Derivation follows Epoch AI's published AI companies documentation
Note
Anthropic's revenue doubles every 3.5 months, the steepest series in the dataset
